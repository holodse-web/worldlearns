# Changelog

## 1.2.0

### Added

- Sworn enemies, raids on your home and the Great March of united settlements.
- Captives and interrogation, mercenaries, village allies, frame-up banners and knowledge hearths.
- 30 new mob skills. The Ender Dragon, Wither, Warden and Elder Guardian adapt to how players defeated them.
- Mobs remember recent conversations and can recall the last exchange when asked.
- A master switch for settlements that keeps mob learning active, separate world-event settings, an Enemies screen, a wiki section and 11 advancements.
- NeoForge builds for all four Minecraft versions, including the legacy NeoForged build for 1.20.1.

### Fixed

- Endless Jump Boost after creeper explosions.
- Empty houses with no residents.
- Destroyed settlements repeatedly respawning residents.
- Monster settlements incorrectly marking themselves as abandoned.
- Settlements missing from maps.
- Provoking a settlement made it hostile to every player.

# World Learns

**Your world learns from the way you play.**

Fight, build, explore and interact with mobs, and the world gradually picks up new tricks. Monsters develop new tactics, animals learn to defend themselves, and villages and monster settlements grow into communities with their own neighbors, allies and enemies.

> **Beta version — back up your entire world before installing or updating.** Bugs and balance changes are possible. Mobs can place and break real blocks, and settlements leave lasting changes in your world.

## Mobs learn from your actions

Your habits influence which skills develop. Combat, equipment, building and everyday interactions contribute to persistent world and regional knowledge.

- **Zombies** can learn to use shields and armor, dig, build and bridge gaps.
- **Skeletons** develop new bow tactics, dodging and close-range combat skills.
- **Creepers and other monsters** gain new behaviors that change how encounters play out.
- **Animals and villagers** also learn, with defensive skills and optional silly behaviors.

Learning happens gradually. Use the settings to adjust learning speed, difficulty, combat skills and humorous skills to suit your world.

## Settlements with a life of their own

Villages expand with homes, paths and shared spaces. Zombie, skeleton and creeper settlements can establish themselves as their building skills develop, then grow around their leaders.

Trade with settlement leaders, while neighboring settlements may form alliances, merge or go to war. Villagers gather wood, plant trees, gossip about nearby communities and react to how you treat them. You can also help villagers learn defensive skills through crafting questions.

Construction and digging respect the `mobGriefing` game rule.

## Follow your world's progress

Press **K** to open **Mob Knowledge**. Browse learned skills, check progress, read the world journal, inspect settlements and open the in-game wiki. The pause menu also provides access to the knowledge screen.

The interface supports **English, Russian, Ukrainian, German, French and Spanish**, with a separate language selector for the mod.

## Installation

1. Back up your world.
2. Install **Fabric Loader** and **Fabric API** for your Minecraft version.
3. Download the matching World Learns JAR and place it in your `mods` folder.
4. Launch the game and check the world settings before playing.

Beta builds are available for **Minecraft 26.3, 1.21.4, 1.21.1 and 1.20.1**. Each requires its own matching JAR; this is a **Fabric** mod.

For multiplayer, install the matching mod and Fabric API on the server and clients for the complete experience. Dedicated-server and mixed-client behavior still needs in-game validation. Custom mobs added by other mods do not automatically receive learning skills.

## Optional AI villager dialogue

Normal gameplay and built-in villager replies work without an AI account or API key. An optional integration with an OpenAI-compatible service or a local model is **disabled by default**.

If the world owner or server operator enables it, messages addressed to villagers, recent conversation context, player and villager display names, and the villager's profession and attitude are sent to the configured endpoint. These names and messages are not anonymized. The default remote preset is **Groq**; **OpenRouter** and local **Ollama** presets are also available. A remote provider processes this data under its own terms and may charge for requests. Review the selected provider's privacy and data-processing terms before enabling it. Server operators should inform players before enabling this feature. Leave it disabled to use the built-in replies.

## Before you play

This is an evolving beta. Compatibility with every modpack is not guaranteed, especially with mods that change mob behavior or protect terrain. Keep a backup before adding, updating or removing the mod. Resetting learned skills does **not** restore blocks changed by construction.

When reporting a problem, include your Minecraft version, World Learns version, Fabric Loader and Fabric API versions, other installed mods, steps to reproduce, and the relevant log or crash report. Remove API keys and other private information before sharing files.
